The Origin

Where the
clouds meet
the soil

Nestled at 1,100 meters in the Western Ghats, our estate sits in Kodagu — India's undisputed coffee capital. Arabica and robusta grow beneath a canopy of silver oaks and rosewood, nurtured by monsoon rains and ancient volcanic soil.

Three generations of cultivation have refined our approach: shade-grown, hand-picked at peak ripeness, and sun-dried on raised beds — preserving every note of terroir in your cup.

From Tree to Cup

Crafted at
every step

Great coffee is not an accident. Every stage of our process is deliberate, unhurried, and guided by a single obsession: to deliver the purest expression of Coorg's landscape.

01

Shade-Grown

Our arabica grows under a canopy of silver oaks and jackfruit trees, slowing cherry maturation and concentrating sugars and complex flavour compounds.

02

Hand-Picked

Cherries are selectively hand-picked at peak ripeness — only the deep crimson ones. Our team moves through the estate three times per season for maximum quality.

03

Natural Process

We use a traditional sun-drying method on raised African beds. Cherries are turned every few hours to ensure even drying over 25–30 days.

04

Micro-Roasted

Our small-batch roastery transforms green beans into their final character. Each origin is roasted to a unique profile that honours the terroir.

Our Responsibility

Grown with
the land,
not from it

Our estate is a living ecosystem. The same canopy that gives our beans their extraordinary complexity also shelters 140+ species of birds, filters the watershed, and cools the microclimate for miles around.

Shade-Grown Canopy

Every tree grows under a protective canopy that sequesters carbon and supports native bird species.

Water Stewardship

Natural processing methods use up to 90% less water than washed processing.

Solar-Powered Roastery

Our on-estate roastery runs on solar energy, reducing our carbon footprint to near zero.

Zero-Waste Pulping

Coffee pulp and husks are composted back into the estate, creating a closed-loop nutrient cycle.

Our Heritage

Three generations.
One devotion.

Our story begins in the mist-laden hills of Kodagu, Karnataka — where shade-grown coffee has been a way of life for over a century.

Timeline
1897

The First Seeds

Our great-grandfather planted the first Arabica seedlings on 40 acres of forested hillside in Kodagu, inspired by traditional Coorg coffee cultivation practices.

1952

Second Generation

The farm expanded to 120 acres under our grandfather's stewardship. He pioneered shade-growing techniques that became standard across Kodagu.

1989

Transition to Organic

We became one of the first certified organic coffee farms in Karnataka, eliminating all chemical pesticides and embracing biodynamic soil management.

2018

The Roastery Opens

We installed our first small-batch drum roaster in Madikeri, closing the loop from farm to cup and launching direct-to-consumer relationships.

2025

Muscat, Oman

Coorg Coffee Roastery opens at The Gate Mall, Al Ghubra North, Muscat — bringing the essence of Kodagu to the Middle East.

Shade-Grown Legacy

Every coffee plant grows beneath a canopy of silver oak and pepper vines, preserving biodiversity and producing beans of extraordinary complexity.

Zero Chemical Inputs

Since 1989, certified organic. We use compost, green manure, and natural pest control — because great coffee starts with healthy soil.

Small-Batch Roasting

Our micro-roastery processes in small batches to ensure precision, consistency, and the highest expression of each bean's terroir.

Direct Trade

We pay fair wages and build long-term relationships with the 40+ farm workers who are the backbone of everything we produce.

Water Stewardship

Rainwater harvesting and closed-loop wet-processing stations mean our footprint on Coorg's precious freshwater is minimal.

Forest Partnership

We actively maintain wildlife corridors adjacent to our land, supporting the leopard, hornbill, and endemic flora of the Western Ghats.
